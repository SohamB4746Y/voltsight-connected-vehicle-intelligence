# Five-minute demo script (live system, nothing staged)

Setup (10 minutes before): open the Codespace, wait for `deploy/live/up.sh` to print the URL, make port 8080 public, sign in once to confirm. For a clean scenario run `deploy/live/up.sh --reset-demo` and wait about 5 minutes: the stressed fleet produces range-risk warnings first, then critical alerts after the charger network degrades at +5 minutes (`DEMO_CHARGER_OUTAGE_AT`). Credentials are printed by the script (not published).

| Time | Say / do | On screen (all from the running backend) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:30 | **Problem.** A fleet manager of EVs needs to know, within seconds of telemetry that changes the answer, whether each vehicle can still reach a charger, and when/where to charge cheaply. | Sign-in screen → Keycloak login as the dispatcher (Authorization Code + PKCE). |
| 0:30-1:15 | **Architecture.** Simulator → mTLS gateway → Kafka (64 partitions) → stream worker with the range-risk engine → alert service → PostgreSQL/Redis → API/SSE → this console; ClickHouse for history; pgvector for incident retrieval; Keycloak, Vault. | Platform page or `docs/architecture.md` diagram. State the scale honestly: this demo streams ~800 events/s; the 100K-vehicle simulator and its measurements are separate. |
| 1:15-2:15 | **Live fleet and map.** Real basemap, vehicles coloured by backend risk state, chargers by live status; markers move every few seconds; the header shows LIVE. Switch city (Chennai) and watch the map fly and the markers filter. | Dashboard cards updating about every 3 s; clusters expand when zooming. |
| 2:15-3:15 | **Risk detection and alerts.** A vehicle turns amber/red and an alert appears in the feed without a refresh. Click the vehicle: SoC, usable range, nearest reachable charger, distance, margin. Click the alert: evidence and the road route the risk engine computed. | Vehicle panel and "Alert evidence" panel with the A* route drawn on the map. Acknowledge the alert (audited). |
| 3:15-4:00 | **Charge orchestration and Copilot.** Ask "Which vehicles are at risk of not reaching a charger?": the answer lists vehicles with tool calls and citations; propose a charge plan and approve it (human approval before any write). Say plainly that the provider is the deterministic one unless an API key is configured. | Copilot page: tool calls, citations, pending action, approval. |
| 4:00-4:30 | **Security and tenant isolation.** Sign in as the viewer: acknowledging an alert and the audit log are refused. Sign in as the Coastal dispatcher: no Meridian vehicle exists on the map. Mention JWT validation, RBAC, RLS, audit hash chain, location masking. | `evidence/live-deployment/live_checks.json` (31 checks) and `auth_map_e2e.json`. |
| 4:30-5:00 | **Scale, ML, observability, delivery.** Measured: 98.3K events/s for 60 s on one 4-core host; 3x burst for 40 s with zero loss; the 5 s alert-latency target was not met at that load. GBM vs baselines, Prometheus/Grafana, Docker image + Helm + Terraform validated in CI, kind-cluster check. Limitations are in `docs/compliance/FINAL_SUBMISSION_STATUS.md`. | Close on the dashboard. |

Recording a video: use the same sequence with a screen recorder at 1080p; the three silent clips in `docs/demo/*.webm` were produced by `tests/e2e/record_demo.mjs` and predate the map.
