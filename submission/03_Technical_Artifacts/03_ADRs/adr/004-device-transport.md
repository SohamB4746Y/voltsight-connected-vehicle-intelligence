# ADR-004 Device ingest transport
**Decision:** HTTPS/2 batches over **mutual TLS 1.3**, protobuf (JSON compat path for OEM clouds). The tenant is derived from the client certificate (issued by the Vault PKI per tenant × OEM connector), never from the payload; revocation is checked against the CRL and a registered-fingerprint directory. **Why:** batching amortises TLS and framing at 100K events/s (≈ 99 B/event protobuf measured vs ≈ 550 B JSON), 429 + Retry-After gives explicit back-pressure, and mTLS gives device identity with standard tooling. MQTT was considered for constrained devices and is **not built**.
